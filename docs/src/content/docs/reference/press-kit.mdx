**Eyes on what you ship.**

Hibou is alpha, not production-ready. Features may be incomplete and breaking
changes are expected. Please preserve that context when describing the project.

## Icon Roles

| Context | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Site, app, marketplace listing | Solid mark or square app tile |
| IDE activity bar or toolbar | Monochrome outline, light/dark variant |
| 16px browser or IDE icon | Dedicated optical cut with simplified eyes |
| Single-color print | Black or white logomark |

Full logos include outlined lettering and do not require a font installation.
The archive includes horizontal/stacked lockups, mark-only variants, PNG exports,
ICO/SVG favicons, and social/README artwork.

## Usage Rules

- Keep the mark at least 24px tall, or use the dedicated 16px asset.
- Keep a horizontal lockup at least 128px wide; below that, use the mark alone.
- Leave clear space of at least one eye diameter around the visible artwork.
- Preserve proportions, eye placement, and the lowercase wordmark.
- Use the supplied light/dark variants; do not stretch, add effects, or recolor
  the amber eyes.
- Do not imply endorsement, affiliation, or production readiness.

The wordmark is based on Space Grotesk Bold. Font license and full usage notes
are included in the archive. Trademark clearance is not independently certified.
